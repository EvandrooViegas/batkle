PRODUCT SUMMARY — for design
Name: Batkle

WHAT IT IS
An AI tool that creates ready-to-post Instagram content for a business: ideas, copy and fully designed posts and carousels. Everything comes out on brand and stays fully editable. You give it your website; it learns the brand and drafts posts in your language, colours, fonts and logo. You review, tweak and publish.

WHO IT'S FOR
Small businesses, agencies and marketers who post regularly but don't want to design every post: construction firms, fintechs, local service companies, creative studios.

HOW IT WORKS (main flow)
1. Add your brand: paste your website URL. The app researches it and builds a brand profile: services, projects, differentiators, audience, tone of voice, language (e.g. European Portuguese), colours, fonts and logo. Everything is editable.
2. Pick designs: choose visual styles from the Design Library. Each style was learned from a few real reference posts. It is a visual language rather than a fixed template, so every post varies while keeping one look.
3. Get ideas: the app suggests post ideas that rotate across your services, projects and strengths, always in your brand's language and never repeating the same subject.
4. Generate a post: one click produces the copy (headline, slides, caption, hashtags) and the design, as a single post or a carousel (1080×1350). Images are generated by AI or picked from stock and your gallery.
5. Edit and export: every element (text, shapes, images, logo) stays editable in a canvas editor. Export as images.

MAIN SCREENS
- Home / Studio: your brands (workspaces) and recent posts.
- Brand Information: the researched brand profile, logo versions (original, white, black, transparent), colours and fonts.
- Brand Personalization: pick the designs this brand uses; previews show each design in the brand's own colours, fonts and logo.
- Creation: an idea list, then for each idea a step-by-step generation card (Content → Visuals → Build) with the finished post preview.
- Gallery: the AI-generated transparent PNG "cutouts" made for the brand's posts (clean ones only), saved so later posts reuse them instead of generating again. People's own photos are added to each post idea instead (4 a post on Free, 8 on Pro, 10 on Business).
- Editor: canvas with layers, text styling (fonts, gradients, highlights), shapes, images (crop, masks, filters), carousel pages, an image picker from the gallery, undo/redo and export.
- Design Library (admin): upload 1–3 reference posts. The app "studies" them (layout, type scale, colour roles, imagery, decorations, branding placement), shows sample compositions, and lets you fine-tune and publish the design.
- Renders / API: designs can also be rendered with dynamic data through a single API call.

KEY IDEAS TO COMMUNICATE
- Learns your brand from your website: no long setup.
- Designs learned from real posts you like, then applied to your brand.
- Every post is different, but the look stays consistent.
- Writes in your language and tone; posts are grounded in your real services and projects.
- AI images (transparent cutouts) plus stock and your own photos.
- Nothing is a flat image: everything stays editable; "the AI drafts, you decide".

CURRENT VISUAL CUES IN THE APP (optional starting point)
- Bold condensed display headings (Bebas Neue style).
- A neon lime accent (#D4FF00) with strong black outlines in the editor.
- Clean light/dark UI, rounded cards.
